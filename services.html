Our Services

K-Plumbing offers a full range of residential and commercial plumbing services. Search below, or get in touch for a free quote.

Leak Repairs & Installs

From dripping taps to hidden pipe leaks, our team quickly locates and repairs leaks to prevent water damage and reduce your water bill. We also handle new pipe and fitting installations for renovations and new builds.

Blocked Drains & Geysers

Slow or blocked drains and faulty geysers are two of the most common household plumbing issues. We offer professional drain unblocking as well as geyser installation, repair, and replacement.

24/7 Emergency Callouts

Plumbing emergencies don't wait for business hours. Our emergency callout service is available around the clock for burst pipes, major leaks, and other urgent issues.

Bathroom & Kitchen Renovations

Planning a renovation? We handle all plumbing aspects of bathroom and kitchen upgrades, from re-piping to fitting new basins, showers, and taps.
